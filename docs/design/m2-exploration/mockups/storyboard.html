From management to adventure transition sketch

1 · PrepareAt Edge of Town the workspace shows a readiness summary computed by Go: members, lineup, status refusals (e.g. a dead member cannot enter). Nothing is auto-fixed.
2 · Enter the MazeThe user chooses Maze (menu, button or ⌘↩). The controller performs the transition; the presentation only animates it.
3 · ArriveCamera blends from the castle view to the team at the stairs, then settles in the user's preferred camera (first person or attached). Input context switches to exploration keys.
4 · ExploreTeam summary stays visible (form undecided). A profile opens read-only with 1–6 or a click; equipment changes wait for Camp, as the rules require.
5 · ReturnStairs up on B1F, or a warp, ends the expedition. The controller returns the team to the Castle; the workspace reopens where the user left it.

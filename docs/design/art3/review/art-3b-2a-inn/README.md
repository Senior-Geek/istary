## The preview's findings (ART-3B-2A)

- **Centred**, the picture is fine at 1440×900; at 980×692 the actions
  cover the sign and, once a guest is chosen, half her face.
- **Kept right** (`100% 50%`), her face and upper body and the whole sign
  are clear at both sizes, story box open or closed, with or without a
  guest; the sign's bed touches the head bar at 980×692. It gives up the
  picture's left 10% (1440×900) or 20% (980×692): the fireplace's left
  side and the reader, mostly behind an open story box at either crop.
- **The story box** covers the left of the room, never her or the sign;
  the corridor keeps the room's depth with it closed. The desk's front
  and the bell are under the information, as the Tavern's bar is.
- **Full** is preferred to Subdued, which flattens the candle and lantern
  light. **Sharpness:** upscaled 1.15×, as the other pictures are.
- **Controls:** at both crops and sizes none clipped, covered or
  overflowing; the rooms, rates and refusals unchanged.

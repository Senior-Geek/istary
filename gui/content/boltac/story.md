# Boltac's Trading Post

The door opens with the cheerful jingle of a brass bell. Warm light spills across polished armour, racks of swords, and shelves crowded with bottles of suspiciously brilliant colours. Somewhere beneath the counter, a cat opens one eye, judges you unworthy of further attention, and goes back to sleep.

Behind a great wooden counter sits a round, white-haired gentleman whose smile grows noticeably wider as he notices your purse.

"Welcome, welcome!" cries **Boltac**, spreading his arms. "The finest weapons, the stoutest armour, the most remarkable magical treasures in all of Trebor's kingdom! And every one of them reasonably priced!"

You glance at a battered shield bearing what looks remarkably like a fresh dragon bite.

"Previously owned," Boltac explains smoothly. "By a gentleman who no longer requires it. Excellent workmanship."

He gestures toward his shelves.

"Buy, sell, identify, remove a troublesome curse — all under one roof! Though I should mention that certain enchantments are rather more difficult to remove than they are to acquire."

He leans forward confidentially.

"Now, should you happen to discover anything interesting in Werdna's Maze, bring it straight to me. I pay a fair price for everything. Well, almost everything."

The cat raises its head at the sound of coins being counted.

Boltac smiles again.

"Remember, my friend: a good sword may save your life. A better sword may save it twice. And if neither does, I'll be delighted to purchase them back from whoever brings them home."

# Gilgamesh's Tavern

The door swings shut behind you, and the chill of the courtyard is gone. Firelight
plays across old timbers. Somewhere a lute is being played, not quite in tune, and
nobody seems to mind.

Every table is taken. Fighters compare scars while a young mage pretends not to
listen. In the gallery above, a priest blesses a bowl of stew, and the thief beside
him quietly counts the spoons.

Behind the bar stands a broad, gray-bearded man with his arms folded and a smile
that has outlasted a great many customers.

"Welcome, welcome," says **Gilgamesh**. "Another one answering Trebor's call, I see.
Sit anywhere you like. Not that table, though. It's kept for a party that went down
to settle matters with Werdna." He polishes a tankard thoughtfully. "Some while ago
now."

He nods toward the crowded room.

"Everyone here means to go down into the Maze, and the wise ones know better than to
go alone. Choose your companions with care. A friend who will carry you home is worth
more than gold. A friend who offers to carry your gold is not quite the same thing."

You notice that good and evil seldom share a table for long. Gilgamesh assures you
they fare even worse sharing a corridor beneath the castle.

"Well, then," says Gilgamesh, setting down the tankard. "Find yourself some good
company. The Maze will still be there in the morning. It always is."

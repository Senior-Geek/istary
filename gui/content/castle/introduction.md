# Welcome to the Castle

You have arrived at the Castle of Trebor, the Mad Overlord, whose lands lie
beneath the shadow of a terrible evil.

Deep below the Castle, the wizard Werdna has hidden himself within a vast and
treacherous Maze. There he guards a powerful amulet stolen from Trebor himself.

The Overlord has called upon adventurers from every corner of the realm. Riches
and glory await those brave enough to descend into the labyrinth, defeat
Werdna, and return with the stolen amulet.

Many have answered the call. Few have returned.

Now it is your turn.

But no adventurer should face the Maze alone. You must first gather a company
of companions, each bringing their own strengths, skills and knowledge of
magic.

**Your journey begins at the Edge of Town.**

There you may establish a new adventure and visit the Training Grounds, where
warriors, priests, mages and thieves begin their journeys.

Create your characters, choosing their names, races, professions and
abilities. Then return to the Castle, where Gilgamesh's Tavern provides a
gathering place for your party, Boltac's Trading Post offers equipment, and the
Adventurer's Inn provides rest.

When your company is assembled and prepared, the Maze awaits.

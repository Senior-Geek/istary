# The Adventurer's Inn

The heavy oak door closes behind you, shutting out the noise of the castle courtyard. Warmth, candlelight, and the comforting smell of fresh bread greet you. Across the hall, a fire crackles, and the distant sound of laughter drifts down a corridor.

Behind a polished wooden desk stands a smiling woman in a blue-and-gold gown. An enormous ledger lies open before her, beside a brass bell and a quill and inkwell.

"Welcome to the Adventurer's Inn," she says. "I'm **Mistress Elspeth**. You look as though you've come from the Maze. Or are about to. It's sometimes difficult to tell."

She glances at your companions with a practiced eye.

"Stables are free, of course. A little straw, a roof overhead, and time enough to recover your spells. But if you've acquired a few injuries, I recommend a proper bed. We have accommodations for every purse, from a modest cot to the Royal Suite."

She pauses, turning a page in her ledger.

"Naturally, the better the room, the quicker the recovery. Gold has a remarkable effect on one's health, I've found."

Her smile softens.

"And should one of your companions be in a condition that sleep cannot remedy, I'm afraid you'll need the Temple of Cant. We provide beds, not miracles."

She reaches for her quill.

"Now then, who's staying, and how long?"
